# Session log — 20 October 2026

First build day. nouveaubot went from nothing to a working agent that reads a
bid packet and talks about it. It cannot yet fill a form, and the reason is
data rather than code.

## Why we started

Vin Speranza forwarded the New Bedford Housing Authority solicitation
NBHA-26-150 that morning, with a note arguing the paperwork is heavy enough
to be worth skipping — and that if the sales team worked with IT, much of it
could be filled from previous bids, which would let Nouveau submit many more
bids than it does now. That is the whole brief.

## What the packet turned out to be

239 pages. Roughly twelve of them need ink.

| Pages | What |
|---|---|
| 1–51 | The bidding section. The only part that matters. |
| 52–239 | Contract forms, Section 3 policy, general conditions, 60+ pages of prevailing wage tables, 32 pages of technical specification. Read-only. |
| 144, 176–177 | The only pages carrying real PDF form fields — and all three are post-award certified payroll, not bid forms. |

The submission checklist on page 6 names fourteen required items. Nine can be
filled from company data. Five cannot: the bid bond comes from a surety, two
forms need a notary, and the DCAMM certificate and update statement are
issued by the Commonwealth.

Pages 27–40 (HUD 2530 and HUD 5369-A) extract as garbage because of damaged
font encoding, so they have to be read visually rather than as text.

### The deadline contradicts itself

Still unresolved, and it blocks planning:

- Page 1: "2:00 p.m. **Thursday October 26, 2023**" — wrong year, and 26 October 2026 is a Monday
- Pages 4 and 10: "2:00 PM **Thursday, October 29, 2026**" — internally consistent

Two sources say the 29th, one stale cover page says the 26th, and Vin's email
says the 26th. Nobody but Kevin Rego at the Authority can settle it, and it
needs to be in writing. The agent finds this contradiction on its own and
refuses to pick a side.

## What we built

### The agent

nouveaubot runs on Hermes Agent v0.22.2 from Nous Research, configured as
**nouveauhermes** and thinking with Gemini. Hermes owns the loop, the tools,
the skills, and the memory.

Hermes does not fine-tune. "Grows with you" means procedural memory: it
writes skills from experience and searches its own past sessions. That turned
out to be the right shape anyway — a model with tax identification numbers
baked into its weights would generate plausible ones from memory, onto forms
carrying perjury clauses.

Its identity lives in `~/.hermes/SOUL.md`, and the `nouveau-bids` skill holds
the bidding procedure and scripts.

### The interface

A Next.js chat at `localhost:3000`: conversation history, markdown rendering,
multi-file attachment with drag and drop, elapsed-time feedback while the
agent works, and a company profile panel.

It reaches the agent over Hermes' OpenAI-compatible gateway on port 8642, so
the browser and the terminal talk to the same agent with the same memory.

### The rule everything is arranged around

A value reaches a bid form only when a person has marked it confirmed. The
model decides what to do; it never supplies a company fact.

The profile at `~/.hermes/nouveau/profile.json` stores every fact with a
confidence and the place it came from. Unconfirmed values are shown for
review and are never printed.

## What we proved works

- Fed the real 239-page packet through the agent: it found 13 required
  submittals, separated what it can prepare from what a person must obtain,
  and flagged the deadline contradiction with page numbers, unprompted.
- Attached two files at once; it read both and quoted a fact from each.
- Wrote a fact through the web app and read it back through the agent's own
  script. One store, two doors.

## What does not work yet

**No form has been filled.** `fill_form` is stubbed. Filling needs field
coordinates, since the bid forms carry no PDF form fields, and those maps are
built once per form from a real packet.

**The profile is empty.** 0 of 41 facts confirmed. This is the binding
constraint: no amount of further code produces a filled form without it.

## Course corrections worth remembering

We built a file-upload tool first when the ask had been a chat bot from the
opening message. Rebuilt as chat.

Hermes was installed but the existing TypeScript agent loop was left in
place, so for a while there were two brains and the browser was talking to
the wrong one. The TypeScript loop was deleted and the app now proxies to
Hermes.

The agent then answered "what can you do" by listing iMessage, ASCII video,
and p5.js art, because it had no identity. SOUL.md fixed that.

The sidebar's profile counter was hard-coded to "0 of 40" and read nothing.
It now reads the real file.
